Words that look Italian… but aren’t. Learn the trickiest ones before they land you in trouble.

The rule.

False friends are words that look very similar in Spanish and Italian but mean something else. Because the two languages are cousins, there are loads of them… and some can land you in an awkward spot.

And some are real friends: exquisito = squisito, cena = cena (dinner).

The trap for Italian speakers.

✗Estoy muy embarazada por mi español.

✓Me da mucha vergüenza hablar en español.

«Imbarazzata» (embarrassed) is avergonzada or «me da vergüenza». Embarazada means you’re expecting a baby.
