I understand almost everything… but when I have to speak, I freeze.

It happens to almost all my students. Understanding and speaking are two different things.

That’s why in class you speak first and then we correct together.

Understanding isn’t speaking.

With books and apps you learn to recognise Spanish. In class you learn to use it.

Studying alone

  • You understand it, but you freeze when you speak.
  • You repeat the same mistakes without noticing.
  • Textbook grammar, far from real Spanish.
  • You start keen… and give up after a few weeks.

In class with me

  • You speak from the first minute, without fear of making mistakes.
  • I correct you on the spot and explain why.
  • Materials and games made to measure.
  • A fixed appointment that keeps you going.

  2. A2You talk about your daily life and what you’ve done.

  4. B2You talk fluently about almost any topic.
