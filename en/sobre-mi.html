Spanish teacher, from Granada, in Rome. I help people who want to learn Spanish take the hardest step: speaking it without fear.

  • 500+hours of teaching

My story.

  1. Granada

    Where it all begins

    Spanish is my mother tongue. I grew up in Granada and I’ve always been fascinated by languages and the way we communicate.

  2. Rome

    I learnt Italian on my own

    When I moved to Italy, I learnt Italian on my own: I tried materials, methods and techniques, and made lots of mistakes. That’s how I found out what really works to learn a language quickly.

  3. Your teacher

    From helping friends to teaching

    I started by helping friends and acquaintances with their Spanish, turning every lesson into a fun experience. Today I have over 500 hours of teaching and a 5.0 rating on Preply.

What my lessons are like.

Four ideas you’ll find in every lesson.

  • 01

    You speak from the first minute

    The lesson is yours: you do the talking, and I help you say it better.

  • 02

    Made for you

    Conversation, grammar or an exam: I adapt every lesson to what you need.

  • 03

    Practical and fun

    Interactive materials and games I design myself, so learning is never boring.

  • 04

    Specialised in Italian students

    I know where Italian and Spanish are alike, and where they trick you, because I made the journey the other way round. Even so, my lessons are open to everyone, whatever language you speak.
